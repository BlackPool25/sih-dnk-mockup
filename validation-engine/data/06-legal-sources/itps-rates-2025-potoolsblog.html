ITPS Rates 2025 - DOP OM No.CF-71/17/2025-CF-DOP dated 28/10/2025

PoTools October 29, 2025 0

CF-71/17/2025-CF-DOP
Government of India
Ministry of Communications
Department of Posts
(IR & GB Division)

Dak Bhavan, Sansad Marg
New Delhi - 110 001
Dated: 28.10.2025

OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Subject: Regarding the tariff of the International Tracked Packet Service (ITPS) for '40' countries in addition to the existing 45 countries which is presently being served by India Post.

The undersigned is directed to inform you that the competent authority has approved the tariff and weight of the International Tracked Packet Service (ITPS) for an additional 40 countries, thereby extending the service coverage to a total of 85 countries. The tariff will be applicable form 29.10.2025 and has been notified in the Gazette of India vide S.O. 4907(E) dated 28th October, 2025 (Annexed). The below mentioned table specifying the tariff of the above said destination is furnished for your kind reference and necessary action at your end.
