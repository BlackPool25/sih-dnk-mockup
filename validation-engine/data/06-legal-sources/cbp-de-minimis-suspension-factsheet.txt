         Suspension of Duty-Free De Minimis
         Treatment                                                                          Updated 08/18/2025
                                                                                   CBP Publication No. 5129-0825



    Effective Aug 29, 2025, imported goods from all countries that are valued at or below
    $800 will no longer be eligible for de minimis treatment and will be subject to all
    applicable duties, taxes, and fees.
