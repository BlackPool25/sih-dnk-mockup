# Category Doc — Small Brass / Metalware (brass utensils, idols, statuettes, bells, decorative metalware)

**Project:** SIH260113 — DNK Export Enablement · **Doc date:** 2026-08-08
**Confidence convention (corpus-wide):** High 80–95% · Moderate 60–80% · Low 40–60% · Speculative <40%. Items marked ⚑ must be re-verified at ship-time.
**Summary line:** the **lowest-compliance, highest-duty-certainty** category of the four — no destination biosecurity, no plant-material paperwork, no NOC. The real levers are (1) **HS subheading choice** (7418 vs 7419 vs 8306 changes US duty from 3% to Free/4.5–5.8%), (2) **weight-driven postage** (dense-heavy), and (3) the **US S.301 10%** overlay.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **7418** | Table, kitchen or other household articles and parts thereof, of copper (incl. brass = copper-zinc alloy) | brass utensils, plates, bowls, lamps, diyas, dinner sets, puja thalis | **7418 19 21** (brass utensils), **7418 19 22** (copper utensils), **7418 19 30** (other table/kitchen/household), **7418 19 90** (parts) | High |
| **7419** | Other articles of copper (residual; not household-table-kitchen, not sanitary) | brass art-wares, decorative vessels, vases, bells of brass, cast brass ornaments | **7419 99 20** / **7419 99 30** (articles of brass — the workhorse code; 7419 99 30 is the #1 brass-handicraft export code per shipment data), also legacy **7419 80 30/40/90** ⚑ | High |
| **8306** | Bells, gongs and the like, non-electric, of base metal; statuettes and other ornaments, of base metal; photograph/picture frames; mirrors | brass idols/statuettes (Ganesh, Nataraja etc.), trophies, wind chimes, bells | **8306 21 10** (statuettes, plated with precious metal), **8306 21 20** (trophies, plated), **8306 29 10** (statuettes, other), **8306 29 20** (trophies, other), **8306 29 90** (other) | High |

Classification guidance (this is where duty is won/lost):
- **Utensil/tableware → 7418; decorative art-ware → 7419; idols/statuettes/frames/mirrors → 8306.** CBP ruling history confirms brass *statuettes* (idols) classify under 8306.29, brass *sculptural plaques/wall art* under 7419.99.50, and functional tableware under 7418. A brass "art wall-plaque" is 7419, not 8306 — get the physical item right.
- **Plated-with-precious-metal statuettes** (8306.21) vs plain (8306.29) — a minor plating (e.g., silver-tone finish) changes the line and duty.
- **Weight > 2 kg cast statuary** can shift to 7419.10 castings ⚑ (per CBP practice on copper castings) — small DNK parcels rarely hit this.
- **Not here:** precious-metal or imitation-jewellery lines (Ch 71), copper scrap/wire (7403–7408), machinery parts.
- EPCH/VKGUY tables historically list brass handicrafts across 7418 19 10/21/22/29/30/90 and 7419 99 20/30 — corroborates the 8-digit set. **Export policy Free for all finished brass articles.**

## 2. PBE declaration description guidance

- **Mandatory:** material + product type + function. Correct: `Brass diya set (6 pcs), tableware` → 74181930. `Brass Ganesh idol, cast, 400 g` → 83062910. `Brass wind chime, decorative` → 74199930 (or 8306 for bell-type) ⚑.
- **Function drives the heading:** if it's used at table → 7418; purely ornamental → 7419/8306. Say which in the description.
- **Declare plating honestly** ("brass, silver-coloured plating") — this is normal and legal; it only changes the 8306.21 vs 8306.29 line.
- **Value/weight:** brass is heavy — Σ piece weights ≤ parcel weight and the 2 kg ITPS cap (US/AU/CA) bite quickly; say weights per piece.

## 4. Certifications & restrictions

**The honest, factual position:**
- **No NOC, no destination permit, no biosecurity clearance for plain brass/metalware** to USA/UK/UAE/AU. India export policy is **Free**. This is the "generally safe" category — but four factual cautions:
  1. **Composition/consumer-safety laws** apply at the consumer level, not customs clearance: brass often contains **lead (~1.5–3.5% in free-machining brasses)**. For **food-contact** items (utensils, thalis, drinking vessels) the EU/US/AU have heavy-metal release/migration limits for food contact materials, and US CPSIA lead limits apply to *children's* metal items. Adult decorative items: no customs stop, but US CA Prop 65 (lead) and EU REACH lead Entry 63 (500 mg/kg in jewellery-type articles — only if the item is jewellery) matter for retail listing. **Do not present this as an export certificate requirement — it is a market-access/product-safety note.**
  2. **Metal polish/chemicals:** never send brass **polish/liquids/patina chemicals** with the parcel — liquids are restricted in postal (corpus §5.2) and some polishes contain corrosives/flammables (IATA). Ship the metalware only, dry and clean.
  3. **Magnets** in brass wind chimes/magnet-mount decorations → IATA Class 9 rule (see jewellery doc §4.2) if above threshold.
  4. **Antique/archaeological-replica metalware:** items made to look antique are fine, but genuine antiques (>100 yrs) and idol statuary for religious use have separate destination rules in some countries; not a DNK-scale issue — flag only.
- **Ireland-style wood bans do not apply** (metal, not plant material). Some countries' postal lists restrict *metal with religious/cultural significance*? No evidence — do not invent; the authoritative per-country list is India Post's scanned `Country_List.pdf` (curate top-N, corpus Ctx-4).

## 5. Shipping lane notes

- **Weight profile: dense-heavy.** A brass idol/utensil set hits the **2 kg ITPS cap** (US/AU/CA) fast; 1 kg of brass is a small object.
- **Indicative ITPS postage** (gazette Table VIII): 500 g → USA ₹715, UK ₹425, UAE ₹320, AU ₹800; 1 kg → USA ₹1,065, UK ₹675, UAE ₹470, AU ₹1,250.
- **No volumetric risk** (dense) — EMS/volumetric divisor is a non-issue unless boxed with excessive void.
- **Watch the 2 kg cap:** a 2.1 kg brass parcel to USA/Australia/Canada must go EMS/Air-Parcel (250-g slabs, higher cost). Under 2 kg → ITPS always.
- **Packing:** bubble-wrap + rigid box; brass is hard and heavy — protect corners; no need for plant-material packing restrictions (no straw/soil rules apply to metal, but reuse the no-straw rule for any filler to be safe on AU).
